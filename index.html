    STREAM A · FIBER

    Paper & Mixed
    Paper

    General examples of clean, dry paper. Acceptance depends on the collection program.

    Examples to check

    • Notebook and composition paper
    • Classroom handouts and office printouts
    • Envelopes, flyers, and study cards
    • Non-laminated paper folders

    PREPARATION CHECKS

    • Keep paper dry and free of food residue.
    • Ask staff about wire bindings, staples, and paper clips.
    • Separate mixed packaging when instructed.

    Common mistakes to check

    Thermal receipts, coated paper, used towels, and napkins may need a different disposal route. Do not assume all paper-like items are accepted.

    Plastics &
    Containers

    Bottles, jugs, and rigid containers. The image model cannot read or verify resin codes.

    Examples to check

    • Plastic beverage bottles
    • Milk and detergent jugs
    • Yogurt cups and rigid tubs
    • Caps and lids according to local rules

    PREPARATION CHECKS

    • Empty liquids and remove food residue.
    • Rinse as the collection program requires.
    • Check instructions about caps, labels, and flattening.

    Common mistakes to check

    Plastic bags, film, utensils, and straws may be excluded from the container stream. Ask staff about reuse, trash, or approved specialty collection.

    STREAM C · CORRUGATED

    Cardboard &
    Boxes

    Shipping boxes and paperboard packaging. Keep material clean and dry.

    Examples to check

    • Corrugated shipping and supply boxes
    • Cereal and tissue boxes
    • Cardboard tubes and cores
    • Clean cardboard packaging inserts

    PREPARATION CHECKS

    • Flatten boxes as instructed.
    • Remove foam, plastic wrap, and other inserts.
    • Ask staff about packing tape and coated packaging.

    Pizza boxes and food residue

    Separate clean and soiled portions when the collection program requires it. The model cannot decide whether grease contamination is acceptable.
